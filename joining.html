Joining Grade 10

To join us in Grade 10, use the Ministry of Education’s KEMIS portal for senior school selection and placement. Search for Kasikeu Girls High School using our institution code, CV7S.

Track placement through KEMIS ↗
What should we check in the joining instructions?

Our joining instructions are where you’ll find your reporting date and time, approved fees and payment channels, documents to bring, and uniform and boarding requirements.

What can we ask about before reporting?

Ask our school office about transport, visiting arrangements, and what to pack. If your child needs medication, dietary accommodations, or learning support, speak with us before reporting.

How can we compare subject combinations?

Start with subjects the learner enjoys and wants to pursue. Compare the three subjects in each combination, then check the combination code and current availability in KEMIS.

What if we need help with KEMIS?

The Ministry’s portal lists support@kemis.go.ke for help with selection and placement.
