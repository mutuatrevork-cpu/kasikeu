Our school and history

We are a public girls’ boarding school in Kasikeu, serving families in Mukaa and beyond.

Our history

Rooted in Kasikeu

Our story is rooted in Kasikeu, where we have grown as a girls’ school in Mukaa Sub-County, Makueni County.

From early investment in classrooms to the expansion of our campus, the milestones below tell part of our school’s story. Today, we are a public C3 girls’ boarding senior school.

  1. 2004

    Classroom development

    A classroom construction project at our school received funding through Kilome constituency.

  2. 2009–2010

    Our KCSE candidates

    Thirteen of our students sat the KCSE in 2009, followed by 23 in 2010.

    See our KCSE results
  3. 2018–2019

    Campus expansion

    Work continued on a building for our administration block and three classrooms with support from Kilome NG-CDF.

  4. 2025

    Senior school transition

    We joined Kenya’s senior school register as a public C3 girls’ boarding school.
