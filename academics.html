Choosing your subjects

Start with the subjects you enjoy, the skills you want to build, and the options you may want to pursue after school.

  1. Compare the full combination. Read all three subjects together and consider the practical, reading, and writing work they involve.
  2. Discuss your shortlist. Talk it over with a teacher or family member and note the combination codes that fit your interests.
  3. Check your intake. Confirm the school and subject codes in KEMIS before finalising your choices.
